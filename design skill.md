Design a premium, minimalist, human-centered website in a dark atmospheric
gradient style. Treat it like a cinematic film still turned into a website:
quiet, spacious, emotional, and refined, not a busy SaaS template.

MOOD: Cinematic, analog, calm, intimate. Soft-focus human silhouettes,
film grain, dusk-blue gradients, vast negative space.

COLOR: Background Ink #05080F and Midnight #0A1530. Gradients in Cobalt
#1B3FD8, Electric #2F6BFF and Sky #8FC1FF. Text Paper #F4F6F9 (92% for
headings, 60% for body). One rare warm accent, Dusk #FF8A3D, used only as a
tiny horizon glow. One gradient per section, never competing.

TYPOGRAPHY: Display in a light grotesk (Inter Tight / Geist / Neue Montreal),
weight 300, clamp(56px, 9vw, 144px), line-height 0.95, tracking -0.04em.
Exactly one italic serif word per headline (Instrument Serif Italic).
Micro-labels in mono (Geist Mono) 11px, uppercase, 0.08em tracking, 50%
opacity, placed in corners like [ 01 — Index ]. Body 16–18px, line-height 1.6,
max 56ch.

LAYOUT: 12-column grid, 8pt spacing, 160–200px section padding, hairline 1px
dividers, narrow content column, generous empty space. Centered or left-aligned
hero with one headline, one subline, one CTA.

HERO: Full viewport. Blurred gradient field with a subtle fluted/reeded-glass
vertical-streak texture and 6–8% film grain. A tiny out-of-focus human
silhouette in the lower third, small against the vast space. Transparent
navbar with wordmark, 3–4 links, and one ghost pill CTA.

COMPONENTS: Pill buttons (primary Paper fill, secondary 1px 20%-white border,
48px tall, soft glow on hover). Frosted-glass cards (rgba(255,255,255,0.04),
1px 10% border, 24px radius, backdrop-blur 24px, 32–40px padding, max 3 per
row). Stats row with 3 large light-weight numerals, tiny mono labels, and
hairline dividers. Footer with a giant faded wordmark and tiny mono links.

IMAGERY: Grainy, soft-focus, blue-monochrome photography. Faceless or
backlit figures at small scale. No stock smiling faces, no laptop/phone
mockups, no neon.

MOTION: Slow, cinematic. Gradients drift on a 20s loop, text fades up 24px
over 700ms ease-out, images reveal from blur to sharp. No bouncy or
flashy animations. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

AVOID: Clutter, dense card grids, neon green/purple, emojis, heavy
shadows, bold or heavy font weights, more than one accent color, generic
"AI startup" stock visuals.

DELIVER: A responsive (mobile-first) page with hero, short about statement,
3 feature cards, stats row, one testimonial, CTA section, and footer.
